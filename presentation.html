The Problem

Martial arts students often train without expert supervision. Incorrect form leads to injuries and slow progress. Human coaches can't scale — what if AI could watch every move?

🤕

Injury Risk

Incorrect technique during solo practice can cause joint injuries, muscle strains, and bad habits that are hard to unlearn.

👨‍🏫

Limited Access

Not every student has access to a qualified coach for every training session. Remote learners are especially underserved.

📈

No Tracking

Students have no way to objectively measure their progress, track improvement over time, or identify weak areas.

💡

Our Solution

A web application that turns any webcam into an AI karate coach — classifying moves in real time and telling the user exactly what to fix.

MediaPipe Pose — How It Works

MediaPipe uses Google's BlazePose model to detect 33 3D landmarks on the human body in real time — even on mobile devices.

33 Body Landmarks

IDLandmarkUse in Karate
11–12ShouldersGuard position
13–14ElbowsPunch angle
15–16WristsFist position
23–24HipsRotation, stance
25–26KneesKick height
27–28AnklesKick extension

Why MediaPipe?

  • In-browser — no server GPU needed for pose detection
  • Privacy — video never leaves the user's device
  • 30+ FPS — fast enough for real-time martial arts
  • 3D coordinates — x, y, z per landmark + visibility score
  • Free & open source — by Google

Training Configuration

OptimizerAdam, lr = 0.001
LossCategorical Crossentropy
Batch Size32
Epochs100 (early stopping)
Window Size30 frames (~1 sec)
Features/Frame12 joint angles
Data Split70 / 15 / 15

Why LSTM?

  • Captures temporal patterns — martial arts moves are sequences, not static poses
  • Handles variable speed — different athletes perform at different speeds
  • Proven in action recognition research

Trial Version — Demo Model

Before training the full LSTM on the Kyokushin dataset, we run a trial/demo model to validate the entire pipeline end-to-end.

What the Trial Model Does

  • Generates synthetic data — simulates 33 landmarks with realistic joint angle ranges for each of the 4 karate moves
  • Trains a lightweight classifier — Random Forest on joint angle features (fast, no GPU needed)
  • Classifies in real time — takes MediaPipe landmarks from webcam, computes angles, predicts the move
  • Proves the pipeline — webcam → MediaPipe → angles → classification → correction feedback

Correction System

After classifying the move, a rule-based correction engine compares user's joint angles to ideal reference values and generates feedback.

How It Works

① AI classifies the move (e.g. "Front Kick")
② System looks up ideal joint angles for that move from the database
③ Compares user's angles vs. ideal — if difference > 15°, generate a correction
④ Shows correction text + highlights the joint in red on the skeleton overlay

Example Corrections

🦵 Front Kick:
"Raise your knee higher — aim for waist height before extending"
👊 Reverse Lunge Punch:
"Rotate your hip more — power comes from hip rotation, not just the arm"
🌀 Roundhouse Kick:
"Turn your supporting foot outward — pivot on the ball of your foot"
